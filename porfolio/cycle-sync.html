Chrome Extension

Cycle Sync

Import your period data from Apple Health or any CSV export, and push it straight to Google Calendar — with smart predictions for the next 6 months.

Install on Chrome — it's free Privacy policy

How it works

1
Export your data Apple Health XML or a CSV from Clue, Flo, or Natural Cycles.
2
Upload to Cycle Sync Drag in the file. Everything is parsed locally — nothing leaves your device.
3
Sync to Google Calendar Historical days go in as past events. Predicted windows appear as RSVP invites — accept if your period arrived, decline if not.

What's included

Apple Health XML CSV import 6-month predictions Per-day events RSVP tracking One-click cleanup 100% local — no server
