How it works

Three steps to your calendar

1
Export your health data
From Apple Health: Settings → Health → Export. From Clue, Flo, or Natural Cycles: use their CSV export option.
2
Upload to Cycle Sync
Open the extension, drop your file.
3
Sync to Google Calendar
One click adds all events. Predicted days appear in blood red with an RSVP invite — accept when your period arrives, decline if it doesn't come.
